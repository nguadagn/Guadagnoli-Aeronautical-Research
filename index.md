# Guadagnoli Aeronautical Research

## Ornithopter R&D Project

This project focuses on the research and development of flapping-wing aircraft (ornithopters).

## Research Areas
- Aerodynamic modeling of flapping wings
- Structural design of wing systems
- Actuation mechanism development
- Flight performance analysis

## Methods
- MATLAB simulations
- CAD design
- Aerodynamic analysis
- Prototype development
